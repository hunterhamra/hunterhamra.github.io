"I’m Hunter, based in Adelaide, with over 10 years of experience across operations, fashion and retail, eCommerce, customer experience, and more recently, the NDIS sector.

I’m naturally someone who likes to understand how things work and, more importantly, how they could work better. My strengths sit across operations, systems, compliance, project coordination, communication and problem-solving. I enjoy taking something that feels complicated or disorganised and turning it into something clear, practical and manageable.

Alongside my career, I’m studying psychology, which has strengthened my interest in people, behaviour and the way we think. I love being able to combine that curiosity with the practical, creative side of my personality.

Outside of work and study, I’m usually working on some kind of creative project—sewing, embroidery, crafting, gardening or learning a completely new skill because I’ve decided I absolutely need to know how to do it!

At my core, I’m curious, creative and organised. I enjoy learning, solving problems and taking ownership of projects, and I’m happiest in roles where I can bring together people, ideas and systems to make things work better."
