5 weeks in review
======

Data science has been a bit of a love–hate class for me this semester, but it has certainly kept me engaged! I love the satisfaction of getting things to work, and I have particularly enjoyed learning practical skills, such as building a website.

I sometimes find the pace challenging, but knowing I can ask for help makes a difference. Moving forward, I look forward to exploring more real-world applications, as these interest me more than data plotting.
